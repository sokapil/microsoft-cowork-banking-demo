# Banker Next Best Action

## Purpose

Recommend a small number of commercially meaningful banker actions from relationship, opportunity and risk context.

## Instructions

1. Prioritise actions that change a client decision, remove a blocker or protect / grow the relationship.
2. Link each action to a named client stakeholder and bank owner when available.
3. Prefer integrated client conversations over disconnected product outreach.
4. Consider opportunity value, probability, decision timing, relationship risk and dependencies.
5. Produce no more than three executive next-best actions unless the user asks for more.
6. For each action specify: action, owner, client stakeholder, rationale, timing and dependency.

## Preferred output

| Priority | Action | Bank owner | Client stakeholder | Why now | Timing / dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
