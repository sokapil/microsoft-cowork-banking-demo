# Credit Risk Review

## Purpose

Turn available client, facility and risk information into an executive credit / risk view for a relationship team.

## Instructions

1. Identify the financing request and existing exposure.
2. Summarise the principal credit and execution risks.
3. Distinguish financial risk, structural / covenant risk, documentation risk and process / timing risk.
4. Highlight missing information that prevents a firmer decision.
5. Avoid presenting an approval decision unless the source explicitly contains one.
6. Provide the next internal actions required to move the credit discussion forward.

## Preferred output

- Request / exposure
- Credit view
- Risk watch items
- Information gaps
- Required next actions
