# CIB Relationship Briefing

## Purpose

Create a concise, evidence-led executive relationship brief for a corporate banking client.

## Use when

The user needs a current account view before a client, coverage or leadership meeting.

## Instructions

1. Summarise relationship health in 3-5 sentences.
2. Identify the most important recent engagement signals from available Microsoft 365 / relationship context.
3. List live opportunities in a compact table when three or more are present.
4. Identify key client decision makers and relevant bank owners.
5. Highlight weakening relationship momentum, competitive threats or execution gaps.
6. Separate facts from interpretation. Do not invent missing information.
7. End with no more than three relationship priorities.

## Preferred output

- Overall relationship assessment
- Live revenue opportunities
- Key decision makers
- Relationship / momentum watch items
- Top relationship priorities
